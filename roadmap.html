Thirteen Weeks

Every concept on Gradient Path, in the order the cards actually build on each other — 199 cards across five modules, plus the 18 Blind 75 patterns running alongside. Roughly two and a quarter cards a night. Tick them off as you go; the page remembers.

How to run it

  1. One sitting a night, ~25 minutes. Two or three cards, not more — the cards are written to be read before bed, not crammed.
  2. Read Simple first even when you know the topic. If the plain version surprises you, you did not know it.
  3. Cover the drill answer and say it out loud before revealing. Silent reading feels like learning and is not.
  4. Blind 75 runs in parallel, one pattern every few nights — it is a separate muscle from the concept work.
  5. A week you fall behind on is a week you repeat. The order matters more than the pace.
