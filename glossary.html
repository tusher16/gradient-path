A/A test

Run the experiment with both arms identical. Any 'winner' you see is your false-positive rate showing up.

Comes upAsked when you claim your experiment platform works. At α = 0.05 an A/A test should 'win' about 5% of the time; much more than that means the platform is broken.

How common the thing is before you see any evidence.

Comes upThe quantity that gets dropped in a Bayes question. Worked arithmetic, taking 99% as both sensitivity and specificity for a 1-in-10,000 disease: per million people, 99 true positives against 9,999 false ones, about 100 to 1.
