Chips and Gaps

Every card ends with chips — the concepts it claims to unlock. Resolve each chip against the card that teaches it and you get a map of how the material actually connects. Resolve the ones that match nothing and you get an honest list of what is not written yet.

Read this before the picture. Of 1,274 chips, 182 name another card — 14%. The other 1,033 are vocabulary: terms a card expects you to recognise, and 825 of them appear exactly once in the whole corpus. So the map below is real but deliberately sparse, and the table of uncovered terms is the more interesting half.
