      Where the marks are

      Bar length is each domain’s share of the exam. The teal fill is your accuracy in it.

      The 14 days

      Week 1 teaches all five domains and ends on Mock A. Week 2 drills them in order of weight and ends on a second, unseen mock; day 14 is the exam. Change the start date beside today’s plan if you begin later.

        Thirty topics, by domain

        Each topic reads plainly first, with one line to remember and the trap the exam sets. The exam-level facts sit beside it; Plain only at the top hides them. Listen reads a topic aloud, with its exam facts unless Plain only is on; Listen to today’s topics plays the whole day back to back. The two prompts turn any chatbot into a study partner, written for voice mode so you can practise while doing something else.

        Two mock exams

        Each mock is 65 original questions split 13, 16, 18, 9 and 9 across the five domains, the published domain weights rounded to 65 (AWS publishes no per-question split, and 15 of the real exam’s questions are unscored and unmarked). Each is timed at 90 minutes, with no feedback until you submit. Leaving does not pause the clock. The two mocks share no questions. Once you sit one, its questions join the practice console.

        The real exam reports a scaled score from 100 to 1,000 and passes at 700. AWS does not publish how raw answers convert to that scale, and 15 of its 65 questions are unscored, so the percentages here are not a score prediction. A working rule: clear 80% on both mocks, then sit the real one.

        Prompts for practising away from the page

        Paste one into Claude, ChatGPT or any chatbot, then switch to voice mode. They keep the AI to short turns and one question at a time, and tell it to say so rather than invent an AWS feature.

        Scope and weights follow the official AIF-C01 exam guide, version 1.2 (29 October 2026). AWS says guide updates reach the live exam about a month after they are published, so the newest items may not appear on an exam booked this month. Learn them anyway.
